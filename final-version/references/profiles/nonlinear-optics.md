# Nonlinear Optics Planning

Do not infer a nonlinear model from the phrase "nonlinear optics".

Extract or obtain:

- field equations and dependent variables;
- dimensionality and approximation: full wave, envelope, coupled mode, or ODE/PDE;
- material dispersion and nonlinear coefficients;
- tensor convention and units;
- pump/source normalization;
- boundary and initial conditions;
- phase mismatch and loss;
- solver/study sequence;
- paper observable and comparison tolerance.

The general runner can express nonlinear models through explicit COMSOL physics interfaces and feature properties. Add a reusable adapter only when the generic feature graph cannot represent a required COMSOL operation.

Prefer a paper figure with complete parameters and an analytic or digitizable baseline. Record every assumption separately from paper-derived values.
